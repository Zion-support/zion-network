Use the network without losing your place

  1. Search the registry for the process you want to improve.
  2. Open the app detail to see its source and suggested related tools.
  3. Use synthetic or anonymized examples. Never paste credentials or sensitive client data.
  4. Record expected output, actual output and a human decision: build, wait or do-not-build.
  5. For any paid work, obtain a written scope, client confirmation, applicable PO and Zion authorization before purchasing or dispatching.

Understand status honestly

Registered means an ID exists in the audited catalog. Published requires a deployed URL check. Working requires a functional test. Production-ready requires additional security, reliability and operating evidence. One label never implies the others.

Do not describe modeled savings as achieved savings or provider acceptance as confirmed inbox receipt. Hosting and email services can experience outages.
