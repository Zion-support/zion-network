# 📣 Spotlight: Compliance & GRC AI Suite (2026-09-26)

Audit season shouldn't be a scramble. Zion's **Compliance & GRC AI** suite — 8 free, open-source apps — keeps controls scanned, vendors screened, evidence collected and SLAs guarded, all interlinked.

## The suite
- **[Zion AI Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-compliance-scanner/)** — Automated compliance scanning — CIS benchmarks, policy enforcement, and audit reports. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-compliance-scanner))
- **[Vendor Risk Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-risk-screener/)** — AI vendor risk screening — security, financial, and compliance signals scored. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/vendor-risk-screener))
- **[Audit Readiness Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/audit-readiness-copilot/)** — AI audit-readiness copilot — control gaps, evidence status, and auditor Q&A prep. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/audit-readiness-copilot))
- **[Policy Change Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/policy-change-tracker/)** — AI regulatory & policy change tracking — alerts with impact summaries per jurisdiction. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/policy-change-tracker))
- **[Contract Clause Extractor](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-extractor/)** — AI contract clause extraction — pull obligations, dates, and risks from any contract. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/contract-clause-extractor))
- **[Compliance Evidence Collector](https://ziontechgroup.com/compliance-evidence-collector/)** — AI compliance evidence collection — SOC2/ISO evidence gathered continuously. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/compliance-evidence-collector))
- **[Zion AI Drift Detector](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-drift-detector/)** — Configuration drift detection and auto-remediation for multi-vendor IT fleets. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-drift-detector))
- **[SLA Forecast Guardian](https://ziontechgroup.com/sla-forecast-guardian/)** — Predict SLA breaches before they happen and auto-escalate. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/sla-forecast-guardian))

## Why it matters
- **Continuous**: Evidence collection and compliance scanning run all year, not just before audits.
- **Proactive**: Policy Change Tracker + SLA Forecast Guardian flag risk before it becomes findings.
- **Contractual**: Vendor Risk Screener + Contract Clause Extractor cover third-party obligations.
