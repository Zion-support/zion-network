# 🎓 Education & Learning AI Suite (Batch 80 — 2026-10-04)

Six interlinked apps covering the teaching & learning loop: **align → plan → tutor → track → prove → assess**.

Part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md) · [Hub](../index.html) · [Status](https://zion-support.github.io/zion-status/)

## The loop

1. **[Curriculum Alignment Checker](https://github.com/Zion-support/curriculum-alignment-checker)** — checks units, materials and assessments against curriculum standards automatically and flags gaps ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/curriculum-alignment-checker/)).
2. **[Lesson Plan Generator](https://github.com/Zion-support/lesson-plan-generator)** — standards-aligned lesson plans in seconds, with differentiation, pacing and assessment built in ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/lesson-plan-generator/)).
3. **[Tutoring Session Analyzer](https://github.com/Zion-support/tutoring-session-analyzer)** — analyzes tutoring sessions for engagement, misconceptions and learning outcomes with actionable feedback ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/tutoring-session-analyzer/)).
4. **[Student Progress Tracker](https://github.com/Zion-support/student-progress-tracker)** — tracks mastery and growth per student, class and cohort, surfacing who needs help and where ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/student-progress-tracker/)).
5. **[Learning Outcome Dashboard](https://github.com/Zion-support/learning-outcome-dashboard)** — proves learning outcomes across courses and programs with cohort analytics and exportable evidence ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/learning-outcome-dashboard/)).
6. **[AI Assessment Engine](https://github.com/Zion-support/ai-assessment-engine)** — scored assessments, maturity models and gap analysis for programs and technology adoption ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-assessment-engine/)).

## Why it matters

- **Standards-aligned by default**: curriculum alignment and lesson planning share the same standards graph.
- **Evidence for accreditation**: outcome dashboards export audit-ready evidence per cohort.
- **Intervention that lands early**: progress tracking + tutoring analytics flag at-risk students before exams.
