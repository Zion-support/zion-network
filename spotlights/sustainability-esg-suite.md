# 🌱 Spotlight — Sustainability & ESG AI Suite (Batch 62)

Eight interconnected AI apps for the full ESG lifecycle: collect → account → assess → report → optimize.

## The suite
- **[Carbon Accounting Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/carbon-accounting-hub/)** — Scope 1/2/3 emissions ledgers (GHG Protocol), audit trails, reduction planning · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/carbon-accounting-hub)
- **[ESG Report Builder](https://ziontechgroup.com/esg-report-builder/)** — CSRD, GRI, SASB & TCFD disclosure reports with evidence links · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/esg-report-builder)
- **[ESG Data Collector](https://ziontechgroup.com/esg-data-collector/)** — Automated ESG data ingestion from utilities, ERPs and suppliers · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/esg-data-collector)
- **[Climate Risk Assessor](https://ziontechgroup.com/climate-risk-assessor/)** — Physical & transition climate risk scoring per asset and region · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/climate-risk-assessor)
- **[Circular Economy Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/circular-economy-tracker/)** — Circularity metrics, material flows and waste diversion tracking · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/circular-economy-tracker)
- **[Green Procurement Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/green-procurement-copilot/)** — Sustainable sourcing: supplier ESG scoring and green spend analysis · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/green-procurement-copilot)
- **[Renewable ROI Calculator](https://ziontechgroup.com/renewable-roi-calculator/)** — Solar/wind/storage payback, IRR and incentive modeling · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/renewable-roi-calculator)
- **[Water Usage Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/water-usage-optimizer/)** — Water efficiency benchmarking, leak detection and reuse planning · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/water-usage-optimizer)

## How they interlink
- ESG Data Collector feeds Carbon Accounting Hub and ESG Report Builder
- Climate Risk Assessor scores flow into ESG Report Builder disclosures (TCFD)
- Green Procurement Copilot supplier scores feed Scope 3 ledgers
- Circular Economy Tracker + Water Usage Optimizer metrics feed report KPIs
- Renewable ROI Calculator projects link into reduction planning
