# 🌱 Sustainability & ESG AI Suite (Batch 68 — 2026-10-03)

Six interlinked apps forming a closed loop for corporate sustainability: **measure → audit → optimize → forecast → balance → report**.

Part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md) · [Hub](../index.html) · [Status](https://zion-support.github.io/zion-status/)

## The loop

1. **[Carbon Footprint Tracker](https://github.com/Zion-support/carbon-footprint-tracker)** — ingests energy, travel and procurement data to compute Scope 1–3 emissions baselines. Feeds emission factors to the audit and reporting stages.
2. **[Energy Audit Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/energy-audit-copilot)** — turns the carbon baseline into a prioritized facility audit: anomaly detection on meter data, savings estimates per measure, and capex ranking.
3. **[Energy Optimizer AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/energy-optimizer-ai)** — executes the audit recommendations: HVAC/lighting schedules, demand-response windows and load shifting tuned against tariff curves.
4. **[Renewable Energy Forecaster](https://github.com/Zion-support/renewable-energy-forecaster)** — predicts solar/wind generation so the optimizer schedules flexible loads when clean energy is abundant.
5. **[Smart Grid Load Balancer](https://github.com/Zion-support/smart-grid-load-balancer)** — coordinates multi-site loads and storage against the renewable forecast to shave peaks and maximize self-consumption.
6. **[ESG Report Builder](https://github.com/Zion-support/esg-report-builder)** — closes the loop: assembles CSRD/GRI/TCFD-ready reports from tracker + optimizer telemetry, with evidence links for auditors.

## Why it matters

- **Compliance-ready**: CSRD and EU AI Act-aware reporting pairs with our [AI Governance app](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-governance).
- **Measurable ROI**: every optimization is metered — feed results into the [Zion Tools ROI calculators](https://zion-support.github.io/zion-tools/).
- **Works with field ops**: deploy through our [Zion Field Energy playbook](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-field-energy) for on-site rollouts.
