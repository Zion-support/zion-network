# Spotlight: Healthcare & Life Sciences AI Suite

Part of the [Zion App Network](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-network/blob/main/APPS_NETWORK.md) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com) — 700+ interlinked AI apps.

A connected clinical-operations suite: document, match, comply, flow, bill, schedule — every app links to the others.

## The 8 apps

1. **[Clinical Doc Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-doc-copilot/)** — AI drafting & structuring of clinical notes and discharge summaries. Pairs with [HIPAA Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/hipaa-compliance-scanner/) to keep PHI safe.
2. **[HIPAA Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/hipaa-compliance-scanner/)** — continuous PHI/hipaa posture scanning; feeds findings to [Audit Trail Analyzer](https://ziontechgroup.com/audit-trail-analyzer/).
3. **[Clinical Trial Matcher](https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-trial-matcher/)** — match patients to eligible trials; works with [Care Gap Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/care-gap-radar/) for population outreach.
4. **[Care Gap Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/care-gap-radar/)** — detect and close care gaps across panels; schedules follow-ups via [Telehealth Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/telehealth-scheduler-ai/).
5. **[Patient Flow Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/patient-flow-optimizer/)** — throughput, bed & capacity optimization; coordinates with [Telehealth Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/telehealth-scheduler-ai/).
6. **[Medical Billing Scrubber](https://ziontechgroup.com/medical-billing-scrubber/)** — claim scrubbing & denial prevention; documentation quality from [Clinical Doc Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-doc-copilot/).
7. **[Telehealth Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/telehealth-scheduler-ai/)** — intelligent virtual-visit scheduling, reminders & no-show prediction.
8. **[Pharma Coldchain Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/pharma-coldchain-monitor/)** — temperature excursion detection for vaccines & biologics; compliance evidence for [HIPAA Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/hipaa-compliance-scanner/) audits.
