# 🎙️ Spotlight — Voice, Video & Meeting AI (Batch 41, 2026-09-27)

Every conversation your business has — calls, meetings, webinars, videos — becomes searchable, actionable intelligence with this 8-app suite.

## The suite
- 🎙️ **[Voice Agent Studio](https://ziontechgroup.com/voice-agent-studio/)** — design, test and deploy voice AI agents for inbound/outbound calls.
- 🗣️ **[Voice of Customer Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/voice-of-customer-hub/)** — unify call, chat and survey feedback into VoC themes that drive roadmap.
- 📝 **[AI Audio Transcriber](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-audio-transcriber/)** — speaker-aware transcription for calls, podcasts and video.
- 📋 **[AI Meeting Notes Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-meeting-notes-copilot/)** — decisions, action items and summaries from every meeting, automatically.
- 📹 **[AI Caption Generator](https://ai-caption-generator.ziontechgroup.com)** — instant captions and subtitles, multilingual.
- 📈 **[AI Caption Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-caption-optimizer/)** — test and tune captions for reach, accessibility and SEO.
- 🎥 **[Webinar Funnel Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/webinar-funnel-hub/)** — registration → reminder → replay → nurture, fully automated funnels.
- 📄 **[AI Document Summarizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-document-summarizer/)** — condense hour-long recordings into 2-minute briefs.

## Why it matters
Meetings and calls are the least-mined data in most companies. This suite closes the loop: capture (transcriber, meeting copilot) → package (captions, summaries) → broadcast (webinars) → learn (VoC hub) → automate (voice agents).
