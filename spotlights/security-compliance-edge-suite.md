Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-fraud-detection

## The trust loop
1. **Zion Data Pipelines** delivers governed, lineage-tracked data.
2. **Zion Edge AI Platform** runs the models where the data lives.
3. **Zion Fraud Detection** scores transactions in real time.
4. **Zion Cyber AI** responds to threats and anomalies.
5. **Zion IDP** verifies every human and machine identity.
6. **Zion AI Governance** proves it all to auditors and regulators.
