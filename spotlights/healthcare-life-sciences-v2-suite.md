# 🏥 Healthcare & Life Sciences AI Suite (Batch 79 — 2026-10-04)

Six interlinked apps covering the care delivery loop: **screen → plan → monitor → document → bill → engage**. HIPAA-conscious design, audit-ready logging, role-based access.

Part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md) · [Hub](../index.html) · [Status](https://zion-support.github.io/zion-status/)

## The loop

1. **[Health Risk Screener](https://github.com/Zion-support/health-risk-screener)** — population health risk stratification and preventive-care gap detection ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/health-risk-screener/)).
2. **[Care Plan Generator](https://github.com/Zion-support/care-plan-generator)** — evidence-based, personalized care plans with goal tracking and interdisciplinary collaboration ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/care-plan-generator/)).
3. **[Remote Patient Monitoring AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/remote-patient-monitoring-ai)** — device data ingestion, risk alerting and care-team escalation for continuous remote care ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/remote-patient-monitoring-ai/)).
4. **[Clinical Documentation AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/clinical-documentation-ai)** — ambient clinical note drafting, coding hints and chart-completeness checks ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/clinical-documentation-ai/)).
5. **[Medical Billing Auditor](https://github.com/Zion-support/medical-billing-auditor)** — claim error detection, coding compliance and denial prevention ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/medical-billing-auditor/)).
6. **[Patient Engagement Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/patient-engagement-copilot)** — AI outreach campaigns, appointment reminders and adherence nudges ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/patient-engagement-copilot/)).

## Why it matters

- **Close care gaps**: risk stratification feeds targeted care plans and outreach.
- **Give clinicians hours back**: ambient documentation + coding hints reduce burnout.
- **Protect revenue integrity**: billing audit catches claim errors before denials.
- **Compliance built-in**: HIPAA-conscious design; pair with [Zion AI Governance](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-governance).
