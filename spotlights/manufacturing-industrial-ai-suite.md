Part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md) — [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

An interlinked suite of **9 apps** covering the full industrial loop: **plan → produce → store → move → power → optimize**.

### [Warehouse Optimizer AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/warehouse-optimizer-ai/)
Slotting, picking-path and space optimization for warehouses and distribution centers.

- Repo: [https://github.com/Zion-support/warehouse-optimizer-ai](https://github.com/Zion-support/warehouse-optimizer-ai)

SKU-level forecast models with seasonality and promo uplift detection.

### [AI Supply Chain Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-supply-chain-optimizer/)

## How they interlink

1. **[AI Supply Chain Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-supply-chain-optimizer/)** sets the plan; **[AI Supply Chain](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-supply-chain/)** keeps upstream visibility.
2. **[AI Inventory Forecaster](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-inventory-forecaster/)** + **[Inventory Forecast AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/inventory-forecast-ai/)** turn the plan into SKU-level stock targets.
3. **[Zion Field Manufacturing](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-field-manufacturing/)** executes on the plant floor.
4. **[Warehouse Optimizer AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/warehouse-optimizer-ai/)** handles storage and fulfillment.
5. **[Fleet Fuel Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/)** moves goods efficiently.
6. **[Energy Demand Forecaster](https://ziontechgroup.com/energy-demand-forecaster/)** + **[AI Energy Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-energy-optimizer/)** minimize the energy bill across the whole loop.
