# 🔧 Field Service & Dispatch Ops AI Suite (Batch 81 — 2026-10-04)

Six interlinked apps covering the field operations loop: **quote → cover → dispatch → route → execute → staff**.

Part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md) · [Hub](../index.html) · [Status](https://zion-support.github.io/zion-status/)

## The loop

1. **[Field Service Quote Engine](https://github.com/Zion-support/field-service-quote-engine)** — instant, transparent field service quotes with SLA options and multi-city rollouts, priced in seconds ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/field-service-quote-engine/)).
2. **[Geo Coverage Finder](https://github.com/Zion-support/geo-coverage-finder)** — real-time coverage map of proven field engineers by city, skill and response time ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/geo-coverage-finder/)).
3. **[Smart Dispatch Optimizer](https://github.com/Zion-support/smart-dispatch-optimizer)** — matches engineers to jobs by skill, location, SLA and cost ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/smart-dispatch-optimizer/)).
4. **[Technician Route AI](https://github.com/Zion-support/technician-route-ai)** — multi-stop route optimization with live traffic, parts pickup and SLA windows ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/technician-route-ai/)).
5. **[Onsite Work Order Copilot](https://github.com/Zion-support/onsite-work-order-copilot)** — dynamic checklists, parts verification, safety steps and digital sign-off ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/onsite-work-order-copilot/)).
6. **[Field Tech Marketplace](https://github.com/Zion-support/field-tech-marketplace)** — 10,000+ vetted field engineers and technicians across 50+ countries ([Live](https://ziontechgroup.com/field-tech-marketplace/)).

## Why it matters

- **Quote in seconds, not days** — instant pricing wins deals before competitors respond.
- **Right tech, right job** — dispatch + routing cut windshield time and SLA breaches.
- **Prove every visit** — digital sign-off and parts verification close the loop for billing.
- **Scale on demand** — the marketplace flexes capacity across 50+ countries.
