# Spotlight: Real Estate & Property AI Suite

Part of the [Zion App Network](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-network/blob/main/APPS_NETWORK.md) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com) — 700+ interlinked AI apps.

A connected property-lifecycle suite: value → list → screen → lease → operate. Every app links to the others.

## The 8 apps

1. **[Property Valuation AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/property-valuation-ai/)** — instant AI valuations & comp analysis; feeds pricing into [Listing Description Writer](https://ziontechgroup.com/listing-description-writer/).
2. **[Listing Description Writer](https://ziontechgroup.com/listing-description-writer/)** — MLS-ready listing copy in seconds; pairs with [AI Image Editor](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-image-editor/) for photo-perfect listings.
3. **[Tenant Screening Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/tenant-screening-copilot/)** — fair, fast applicant screening; hands off to [Lease Abstraction AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/lease-abstraction-ai/) at signing.
4. **[Lease Abstraction AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/lease-abstraction-ai/)** — extract clauses, dates & obligations from any lease; renewal alerts feed your [Contract Renewal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/).
5. **[Smart Building Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/smart-building-optimizer/)** — HVAC/energy optimization for portfolios; works with [AI Energy Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-energy-optimizer/).
6. **[Facility Maintenance Predictor](https://ziontechgroup.com/facility-maintenance-predictor/)** — predict failures before they happen; dispatches work via [Field Service Dispatch AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/field-service-dispatch-ai/).
7. **[AI Real Estate Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-real-estate-hub/)** — portfolio command center tying valuation, leasing & operations together.
8. **[AI Real Estate Valuer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-real-estate-valuer/)** — automated valuation models at scale; benchmarks against [Property Valuation AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/property-valuation-ai/).
