# Spotlight: Accessibility & Compliance AI Suite (Batch 67)

Part of the **Zion App Network** — explore the full catalog at [network.ziontechgroup.com](https://network.ziontechgroup.com) and [ziontechgroup.com](https://ziontechgroup.com).

Making digital products accessible and compliant is no longer optional. This suite combines AI-powered WCAG auditing, accessibility statement generation, access governance, and compliance automation into one interlinked toolkit.

## Why This Suite
- **Audit → Fix → Prove**: Checker finds issues, Auditor auto-fixes, Statement Generator produces the evidence.
- **Accessibility + Security together**: pair with Access Review AI for complete governance.
- **Regulatory coverage**: Compliance Auditor and Compliance Guardian cover the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, SOC 2 and more.
