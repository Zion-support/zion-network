# Spotlight: Education AI Suite

Part of the [Zion App Network](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-network/blob/main/APPS_NETWORK.md) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com) — 700+ interlinked AI apps.

A connected teaching-and-learning suite: plan → teach → assess → improve. Every app links to the others.

## The 6 apps

1. **[Curriculum Mapper AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/curriculum-mapper-ai/)** — map standards to units & lessons; feeds course skeletons into [Course Outline Builder](https://ziontechgroup.com/course-outline-builder/).
2. **[Course Outline Builder](https://ziontechgroup.com/course-outline-builder/)** — generate complete course outlines; quizzes auto-drafted by [Quiz Generator AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/quiz-generator-ai/).
3. **[Quiz Generator AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/quiz-generator-ai/)** — assessments in any format from any content; results flow to [Grading Assistant AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/grading-assistant-ai/).
4. **[Grading Assistant AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/grading-assistant-ai/)** — rubric-based grading with feedback; performance signals feed [Student Progress Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/student-progress-radar/).
5. **[Student Progress Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/student-progress-radar/)** — early-warning on at-risk learners; triggers tutoring paths in [Tutoring Copilot AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/tutoring-copilot-ai/).
6. **[Tutoring Copilot AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/tutoring-copilot-ai/)** — personalized 1:1 tutoring aligned to [Curriculum Mapper AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/curriculum-mapper-ai/) standards.
