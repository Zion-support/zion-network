# Mining & Natural Resources AI — Zion App Network Batch 102

Eight interlinked AI apps for mining, oil & gas and natural-resource operators — from pit to port. Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) with a [free online Discovery questionnaire](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/): results are emailed instantly to you **and** to commercial@ziontechgroup.com.

## The 8 apps

- **[Mine Fleet Telemetry AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/mine-fleet-telemetry-ai/)** — Haul-truck and excavator telemetry analytics — utilization, fuel burn and predictive failures. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/mine-fleet-telemetry-ai))
- **[Geological Survey Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/geological-survey-copilot/)** — Summarize drill logs, assay reports and maps into exploration-ready insights. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/geological-survey-copilot))
- **[Tailings Safety Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/tailings-safety-monitor/)** — Continuous tailings-dam risk monitoring with sensor anomaly alerts and GISTM-aligned reporting. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/tailings-safety-monitor))
- **[Commodity Hedging Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/commodity-hedging-copilot/)** — Price scenarios, hedge-ratio suggestions and exposure dashboards for produced commodities. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/commodity-hedging-copilot))
- **[Environmental Compliance Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/environmental-compliance-tracker/)** — Track permits, emissions, water use and rehabilitation obligations with deadline alerts. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/environmental-compliance-tracker))
- **[Drilling Ops Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/drilling-ops-optimizer/)** — Optimize drill patterns and schedules against ore targets, costs and equipment availability. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/drilling-ops-optimizer))
- **[Ore Grade Prediction AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/ore-grade-prediction-ai/)** — Predict ore grades from geological and sensor data to improve blending and mill feed. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/ore-grade-prediction-ai))
- **[Remote Site Logistics Planner](https://ziontechgroup.com/remote-site-logistics-planner/)** — Plan fuel, parts and crew logistics for remote sites with weather and road-risk awareness. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/remote-site-logistics-planner))

## Interlinked workflow
Geological Survey Copilot → Ore Grade Prediction → Drilling Ops Optimizer → Mine Fleet Telemetry → Tailings Safety Monitor → Environmental Compliance Tracker → Remote Site Logistics Planner → Commodity Hedging Copilot.
