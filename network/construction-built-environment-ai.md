# Batch 98 — Construction & Built Environment AI

Seven interlinked, free & open-source AI apps for contractors, builders and construction-tech teams.
All apps cross-link to each other and to the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md). Free Discovery: https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/

## Apps
### blueprint-takeoff-ai
Blueprint Takeoff AI — Quantity takeoffs from blueprints/PDFs — auto-measure areas, counts and lengths into estimate-ready tables.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/blueprint-takeoff-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/blueprint-takeoff-ai
### bid-estimating-copilot
Bid Estimating Copilot — AI bid & estimate generation from historicals, assemblies and market rates with margin guardrails.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/bid-estimating-copilot/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/bid-estimating-copilot
### site-safety-monitor-ai
Site Safety Monitor — Computer-vision PPE & hazard detection on site photos/video with instant safety alerts.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/site-safety-monitor-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/site-safety-monitor-ai
### construction-progress-tracker
Construction Progress Tracker — Photo-to-schedule progress tracking: percent-complete, delay detection and earned-value reports.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/construction-progress-tracker/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/construction-progress-tracker
### subcontractor-compliance-ai
Subcontractor Compliance AI — COIs, licenses & prequal tracking with expiry alerts and automated chase emails.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/subcontractor-compliance-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/subcontractor-compliance-ai
### rfi-router-ai
RFI Router AI — Auto-classify, route and draft answers for RFIs & submittals across the project team.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/rfi-router-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/rfi-router-ai
### equipment-utilization-ai
Equipment Utilization AI — Telematics-driven equipment utilization, idle-cost alerts and rent-vs-own decisions.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/equipment-utilization-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/equipment-utilization-ai
