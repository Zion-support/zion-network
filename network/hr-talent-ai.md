## The suite

### 1. Zion AI Recruiter
Autonomous sourcing and outreach agent that builds pipelines, personalizes outreach and keeps your ATS in sync.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-recruiter/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-recruiter)

### 2. AI Resume Screener
Fair, explainable resume screening with skill extraction, ranking and bias audits.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-resume-screener/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/ai-resume-screener)

### 3. AI HR Talent Platform
Core talent platform: profiles, skills graphs, internal mobility and succession planning.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-hr-talent-platform/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/ai-hr-talent-platform)

### 4. Zion AI Interview Copilot
Structured interview guides, live transcription, scoring rubrics and debrief summaries.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-interview-copilot/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-interview-copilot)

### 5. Interview Prep Copilot
Candidate-side prep with mock interviews, feedback loops and role-specific question banks.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/interview-prep-copilot/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/interview-prep-copilot)

### 6. Onboarding Buddy AI
New-hire onboarding agent: day-one plans, answers, paperwork nudges and 30/60/90 check-ins.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/onboarding-buddy-ai/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/onboarding-buddy-ai)

### 7. Employee Engagement Pulse
Continuous engagement pulse surveys with AI themes, driver analysis and manager action plans.
🌐 Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/employee-engagement-pulse/ · [Repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/employee-engagement-pulse)

## Why it works as a suite
Each app feeds the next: sourced candidates flow into screening; screening signals shape interview guides; interview outcomes seed onboarding plans; onboarding milestones and engagement pulses close the loop on quality-of-hire. One data layer, seven focused tools.
