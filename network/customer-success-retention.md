# Customer Success & Retention AI Suite

Reduce churn, grow expansion revenue and keep every account healthy with this interlinked suite of the [Zion Tech Group App Network](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-app-network). Every app is free, browser-based and open source under the [Zion-support](https://github.com/Zion-support) organization.

## How the suite works together
1. **Onboard** with Onboarding Journey Tracker — stall signals feed Customer Health Scorer.
2. **Listen** via Voice of Customer Hub, NPS Insight Engine and Support Sentiment Monitor.
3. **Predict** churn with Churn Risk Radar and Churn Radar; deflect tickets with Support Deflection Analyzer.
4. **Expand** using Upsell Signal Scout and Partner Referral Tracker.
