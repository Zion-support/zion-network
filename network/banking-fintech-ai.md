# Banking & FinTech AI — Zion App Network Batch 99

Ten interlinked AI apps for banks, credit unions, lenders, payment providers and fintechs — covering the full cycle from onboarding to reporting. Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) with a [free online Discovery questionnaire](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/): results are emailed instantly to you **and** to commercial@ziontechgroup.com.

## The 10 apps

- **[Credit Risk Decisioning AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/credit-risk-decisioning-ai/)** — Score applicants with explainable credit risk models, policy rules and adverse-action reason codes. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/credit-risk-decisioning-ai))
- **[Loan Origination Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/loan-origination-copilot/)** — Automate intake, document checks, underwriting handoff and closing milestones for faster loan cycles. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/loan-origination-copilot))
- **[Open Banking Insights AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/open-banking-insights-ai/)** — Aggregate account data via open banking to surface cash-flow, affordability and product-fit insights. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/open-banking-insights-ai))
- **[Payment Reconciliation AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/payment-reconciliation-ai/)** — Auto-match payments to invoices, flag exceptions and clear suspense accounts across rails. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/payment-reconciliation-ai))
- **[Liquidity Risk Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/liquidity-risk-monitor/)** — Intraday liquidity tracking, LCR/NSFR projections and stress scenarios for treasury teams. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/liquidity-risk-monitor))
- **[RegTech Reporting AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/regtech-reporting-ai/)** — Automated regulatory reporting — data lineage, validation rules and filing-ready outputs. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/regtech-reporting-ai))
- **[Chargeback Defense AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/chargeback-defense-ai/)** — Detect, contest and prevent chargebacks with evidence assembly and representment drafting. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/chargeback-defense-ai))
- **[Wealth Advisor Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/wealth-advisor-copilot/)** — Portfolio insights, rebalancing suggestions and client-ready commentary for advisors. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/wealth-advisor-copilot))
- **[SMB Lending Matchmaker](https://ziontechgroup.com/smb-lending-matchmaker/)** — Match small businesses to the right financing options with eligibility pre-checks and document lists. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/smb-lending-matchmaker))
- **[Banking Data Quality AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/banking-data-quality-ai/)** — Profile, dedupe and remediate core-banking data issues before migrations and audits. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/banking-data-quality-ai))
