# HR & People AI — Zion App Network

Six AI apps for talent acquisition, people analytics and employee growth — part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

## Apps

| App | Live | GitHub | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skills Gap Analyzer | https://ziontechgroup.com/skills-gap-analyzer/ | [Zion-support/skills-gap-analyzer](https://github.com/Zion-support/skills-gap-analyzer) | Maps workforce skills vs. role requirements and flags gaps before they hurt delivery |
