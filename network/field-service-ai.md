# 🧰 Field Service & Support AI — Zion App Network

A suite of AI apps for field service, support operations and hardware lifecycle — built by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

| App | What it does | Live | Repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ticket Triage** | Classifies, prioritizes and routes support tickets with AI | [open](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-ticket-triage/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-ticket-triage) |
| **Field Dispatch Optimizer** | Technician dispatch by skill, geography, SLA and parts availability | [open](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-field-dispatch-optimizer/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-field-dispatch-optimizer) |
| **Spare Parts Matcher** | Matches spare parts to assets, BOMs and compatible substitutes | [open](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-spare-parts-matcher/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-spare-parts-matcher) |
| **RMA Tracker** | Full RMA lifecycle: intake, warranty check, logistics, SLA alerts | [open](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-rma-tracker/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-rma-tracker) |
| **Help Desk** | AI-assisted help desk with suggested replies and KB grounding | [open](https://help-desk.ziontechgroup.com) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-help-desk) |
| **Voice Assistant** | Voice-first assistant for hands-busy field technicians | [open](https://voice-assistant.ziontechgroup.com) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-voice-assistant) |
| **Voice Agent Studio** | Build, test and deploy voice AI agents | [open](https://ziontechgroup.com/voice-agent-studio/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/voice-agent-studio) |
| **Site Survey Planner** | Plan field site surveys and checklists | [open](https://ziontechgroup.com/site-survey-planner/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Zion-support/site-survey-planner) |

## Workflow

1. **Ticket Triage** classifies incoming requests.
2. **Field Dispatch Optimizer** assigns the right technician.
3. **Spare Parts Matcher** confirms parts before the visit.
4. **RMA Tracker** handles returns and warranty flows.
5. **Help Desk / Voice Assistant** keep customers updated.
