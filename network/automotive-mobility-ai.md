# 🚗 Automotive & Mobility AI — Batch 86

Ten interlinked AI apps for fleets, dealerships, EV networks and mobility operators.
Free Discovery questionnaire: https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/ — always online, always free; results emailed instantly to you and to commercial@ziontechgroup.com.

## Apps
- **fleet-health-monitor** — Predictive fleet maintenance — telematics + failure prediction per vehicle. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/fleet-health-monitor
- **ev-charging-planner** — EV charging siting & utilization optimizer for depots and public networks. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/ev-charging-planner
- **dealer-lead-copilot** — Scores dealership leads, drafts follow-ups, books test drives. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/dealer-lead-copilot
- **route-optimization-ai** — Multi-stop routing with traffic, EV range and delivery windows. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/route-optimization-ai
- **driver-safety-coach** — Telematics coaching: harsh events, scoring, training plans. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/driver-safety-coach
- **vehicle-inspection-vision** — Computer-vision inspection reports from photos/video walkarounds. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/vehicle-inspection-vision
- **parts-inventory-forecaster** — Parts demand forecasting to cut stockouts and dead inventory. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/parts-inventory-forecaster
- **mobility-demand-forecaster** — Ride-share/rental demand forecasting by zone and hour. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/mobility-demand-forecaster
- **warranty-claims-triage** — Classifies warranty claims, flags fraud, drafts decisions. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/warranty-claims-triage
- **recall-comms-assistant** — Recall outreach: VIN matching, multilingual owner comms, completion tracking. Repo: https://github.com/Zion-support/recall-comms-assistant
