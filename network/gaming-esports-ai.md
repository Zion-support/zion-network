Ten planned interlinked apps covering the gaming & esports workflow: scout → analyze → balance → broadcast → engage → compete → protect → monetize.

## Planned apps
| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Esports Scout AI** (`esports-scout-ai`) | Ranks unsigned players from ranked-ladder stats + VOD signals. |
| **Match VOD Analyzer** (`match-vod-analyzer`) | Auto-tags rounds, clutch plays and mistakes from match footage. |
| **Game Balance Sentinel** (`game-balance-sentinel`) | Detects overpowered items/agents from win-rate & pick-rate drift. |
| **Live Stream Clipper** (`live-stream-clipper`) | Cuts highlight clips from Twitch/YouTube live streams in real time. |
| **Esports Fan Engagement AI** (`esports-fan-engagement`) | Predicts churn of fans; triggers loyalty quests & rewards. |
| **Tournament Bracket Builder** (`tournament-bracket-builder`) | Seeds brackets fairly, schedules matches, resolves disputes. |
| **Anti-Cheat Anomaly AI** (`anti-cheat-anomaly-ai`) | Flags aim-bot/wall-hack statistical anomalies without kernel drivers. |
| **Streamer Sponsor Matcher** (`streamer-sponsor-matcher`) | Matches streamers to sponsors by audience overlap & brand safety. |
| **Game Economy Modeler** (`game-economy-modeler`) | Simulates in-game currency sinks/faucets; inflation early-warning. |
| **Esports Integrity Monitor** (`esports-betting-integrity`) | Detects match-fixing patterns from odds movement + in-game events. |
