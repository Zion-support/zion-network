## The 8 apps

- **[Game Analytics Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/game-analytics-copilot/)** — Player retention, funnel and monetization analytics with plain-English insights for game teams. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/game-analytics-copilot))
- **[Esports Team Scout AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/esports-team-scout-ai/)** — Scout and compare players with performance stats, role fit and VOD highlight extraction. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/esports-team-scout-ai))
- **[Live Ops Calendar AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/live-ops-calendar-ai/)** — Plan in-game events, seasons and promos with conflict detection and revenue impact estimates. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/live-ops-calendar-ai))
- **[Game Localization Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/game-localization-copilot/)** — Context-aware game string localization with glossary enforcement and LQA checks. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/game-localization-copilot))
- **[Player Support Triage AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/player-support-triage-ai/)** — Classify and auto-draft answers for player tickets — refunds, bugs, bans and account issues. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/player-support-triage-ai))
- **[Anti-Cheat Signal AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/anti-cheat-signal-ai/)** — Surface anomalous gameplay patterns and cluster reports for trust & safety review. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/anti-cheat-signal-ai))
- **[Game Economy Balancer](https://ziontechgroup.com/game-economy-balancer/)** — Simulate in-game economies — sinks, sources, inflation and pricing — before shipping changes. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/game-economy-balancer))
- **[Streamer Sponsor Matcher](https://ziontechgroup.com/streamer-sponsor-matcher/)** — Match streamers and esports orgs with sponsors using audience overlap and brand-fit scoring. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/streamer-sponsor-matcher))
