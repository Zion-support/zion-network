## Support deflection workflow
1. Answer customers instantly with [Help Center Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/help-center-copilot/) grounded in articles maintained by [Knowledge Base Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/knowledge-base-copilot/).
2. Measure what's working with [Support Deflection Analyzer](https://ziontechgroup.com/support-deflection-analyzer/) and find what to write next with [Knowledge Gap Finder](https://ziontechgroup.com/knowledge-gap-finder/).
3. Route what remains with [Support Ticket Triager](https://ziontechgroup.com/support-ticket-triager/) and [AI Email Classifier](https://ai-email-classifier.ziontechgroup.com).
4. Listen at scale with [Support Sentiment Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/support-sentiment-monitor/), [NPS Insight Engine](https://ziontechgroup.com/nps-insight-engine/) and [AI Customer Experience Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-customer-experience-hub/).

## Knowledge retrieval workflow
- Index everything with [AI Neural Search](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-neural-search/) and [AI Document Search](https://ai-document-search.ziontechgroup.com).
- Digest it with [AI Document Summarizer](https://ai-document-summarizer.ziontechgroup.com) and organize it with [AI Document Classifier](https://ai-document-classifier.ziontechgroup.com).
- Capture institutional knowledge from meetings with [AI Meeting Notes Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-meeting-notes-copilot/) and share it via [Report Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/report-scheduler-ai/) and [Onboarding Buddy AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/onboarding-buddy-ai/).
