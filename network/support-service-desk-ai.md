## Why AI for the service desk

- **Deflect before ticket**: better knowledge + smarter search = fewer tickets
- **Triage in seconds**: classification, dedupe, priority and routing automated
- **Protect revenue**: sentiment, churn and upsell signals surfaced to the right team
