# Cybersecurity & Identity AI — Zion App Network Batch 101

Eight interlinked AI apps for security teams, CISOs and IT leaders — from phishing triage to awareness coaching. Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) with a [free online Discovery questionnaire](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/): results are emailed instantly to you **and** to commercial@ziontechgroup.com.

## The 8 apps

- **[Phishing Triage AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/phishing-triage-ai/)** — Auto-analyze reported phishing emails — headers, links, payloads — and draft takedown/user-notice actions. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/phishing-triage-ai))
- **[SOC Alert Summarizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/soc-alert-summarizer/)** — Cluster and summarize SIEM/EDR alerts into analyst-ready incidents with severity and next steps. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/soc-alert-summarizer))
- **[IAM Access Review AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/iam-access-review-ai/)** — Continuous access reviews: dormant accounts, privilege creep and SoD conflicts with remediation drafts. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/iam-access-review-ai))
- **[Vulnerability Prioritizer AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/vulnerability-prioritizer-ai/)** — Prioritize CVEs by exploitability, asset criticality and business context — beyond raw CVSS. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/vulnerability-prioritizer-ai))
- **[Incident Response Playbook AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/incident-response-playbook-ai/)** — Generate tailored IR playbooks and comms templates per incident type, with timeline tracking. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/incident-response-playbook-ai))
- **[Vendor Security Questionnaire AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-security-questionnaire-ai/)** — Auto-answer security questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ, custom) from your control library and evidence. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/vendor-security-questionnaire-ai))
- **[Secrets Leak Detector](https://ziontechgroup.com/secrets-leak-detector/)** — Scan repos, logs and tickets for leaked keys/tokens and orchestrate rotation workflows. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/secrets-leak-detector))
- **[Security Awareness Coach](https://ziontechgroup.com/security-awareness-coach/)** — Personalized micro-training and simulated phishing coaching based on each employee's risk signals. ([repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/security-awareness-coach))

## Interlinked workflow
Phishing Triage → SOC Alert Summarizer → Vulnerability Prioritizer → Incident Response Playbooks → Secrets Leak Detector → IAM Access Review → Vendor Security Questionnaire → Security Awareness Coach.
