# Batch 121 — Banking, Capital Markets & Wealth AI

Seven interlinked, free & open-source AI apps for banks, asset managers, wealth managers and fintechs.
All apps cross-link to each other and to the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md). Free Discovery: https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/

## Apps
### portfolio-insight-ai
Portfolio Insight AI — portfolio analytics, drift alerts and client-ready performance narratives for wealth managers.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/portfolio-insight-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/portfolio-insight-ai
### kyc-onboarding-copilot
KYC Onboarding Copilot — automate KYC/CDD document intake, screening and risk summaries for faster client onboarding.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/kyc-onboarding-copilot/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/kyc-onboarding-copilot
### trade-surveillance-ai
Trade Surveillance AI — detect market abuse patterns (spoofing, layering, insider signals) with explainable alerts.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/trade-surveillance-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/trade-surveillance-ai
### wealth-advisor-copilot
Wealth Advisor Copilot — meeting prep, proposal drafts and next-best-action for advisors, compliance-aware.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/wealth-advisor-copilot/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/wealth-advisor-copilot
### credit-memo-generator
Credit Memo Generator — draft credit memos from financials, collateral and covenant data with risk grading.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/credit-memo-generator/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/credit-memo-generator
### liquidity-forecaster-ai
Liquidity Forecaster AI — daily cash/liquidity forecasting, LCR/NSFR helpers and stress-scenario overlays.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/liquidity-forecaster-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/liquidity-forecaster-ai
### regulatory-filing-copilot
Regulatory Filing Copilot — assemble, validate and track regulatory filings (COREP/FINREP, 10-K/10-Q support).
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/regulatory-filing-copilot/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/regulatory-filing-copilot
