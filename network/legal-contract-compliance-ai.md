## Contract lifecycle workflow
1. Screen inbound contracts with [AI Contract Clause Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-contract-clause-screener/) and [Contract Clause Sentry](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-sentry/).
2. Review and redline with [AI Contract Reviewer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-contract-reviewer/), [Contract Clause Reviewer](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-reviewer/) and [Contract Redline Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-redline-copilot/).
3. Extract obligations with [Contract Clause Extractor](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-extractor/) and track them in [Contract Obligation Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-obligation-tracker/).
4. Never miss a renewal with [Contract Renewal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/).

## Privacy & compliance workflow
- Run assessments with [DPIA Assistant](https://ziontechgroup.com/dpia-assistant/) and [DPIA Generator](https://ziontechgroup.com/dpia-generator/).
- Answer data subject requests with [Privacy DSR Assistant](https://ziontechgroup.com/privacy-dsr-assistant/).
- Enforce retention with [Data Retention Enforcer](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-retention-enforcer/).
- Stay audit-ready with [Audit Readiness Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/audit-readiness-copilot/), [Compliance Policy Writer](https://ziontechgroup.com/compliance-policy-writer/) and [Compliance Evidence Collector](https://ziontechgroup.com/compliance-evidence-collector/).
- Track regulation with [Regulatory Change Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/regulatory-change-radar/), [Policy Change Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/policy-change-tracker/) and [Policy Attestation Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/policy-attestation-tracker/).
