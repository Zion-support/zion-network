## Plan-to-deliver workflow
1. Sense demand with [Demand Sensing Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/demand-sensing-hub/) and forecast inventory with [AI Inventory Forecaster](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-inventory-forecaster/) / [Inventory Forecast AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/inventory-forecast-ai/).
2. Balance stock across depots with [Depot Stock Balancer](https://ziontechgroup.com/depot-stock-balancer/).
3. Book freight at the right price with [Freight Rate Scout](https://ziontechgroup.com/freight-rate-scout/) and schedule docks with [Dock Appointment Scheduler](https://ziontechgroup.com/dock-appointment-scheduler/).
4. Run fleets efficiently with [AI Transportation Suite](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-transportation-suite/), [Route Density Planner](https://ziontechgroup.com/route-density-planner/), [Fleet Fuel Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/) and [Driver Hours Compliance](https://ziontechgroup.com/driver-hours-compliance/).
5. De-risk delivery with [Shipping Delay Alerter](https://ziontechgroup.com/shipping-delay-alerter/) and clear borders with [Customs Doc Generator](https://ziontechgroup.com/customs-doc-generator/).
