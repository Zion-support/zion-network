## Plan → Source → Make → Deliver → Return workflow
1. **Sense demand** with [Demand Sensing Hub](https://ziontechgroup.com/demand-sensing-hub/) and forecast with [AI Inventory Forecaster](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-inventory-forecaster/) and [Inventory Forecast AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/inventory-forecast-ai/).
2. **Balance stock** across depots with [Depot Stock Balancer](https://ziontechgroup.com/depot-stock-balancer/) and buy parts smarter with [Spare Price Scout](https://ziontechgroup.com/spare-price-scout/).
3. **Move freight** at the best rate with [Freight Rate Scout](https://ziontechgroup.com/freight-rate-scout/), clear borders with [Customs Doc Generator](https://ziontechgroup.com/customs-doc-generator/) and get ahead of exceptions with [Shipping Delay Alerter](https://ziontechgroup.com/shipping-delay-alerter/).
4. **Run the fleet** with [AI Transportation Suite](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-transportation-suite/), [Route Density Planner](https://ziontechgroup.com/route-density-planner/), [Smart Dispatch Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/smart-dispatch-optimizer/) and [Dispatch Window Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/dispatch-window-optimizer/); keep drivers compliant via [Driver Hours Compliance](https://ziontechgroup.com/driver-hours-compliance/) and cut fuel spend with [Fleet Fuel Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/).
5. **Optimize the dock and returns** with [Dock Appointment Scheduler](https://ziontechgroup.com/dock-appointment-scheduler/), [AI Returns Reducer](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-returns-reducer/) and [Returns Optimization AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/returns-optimization-ai/).
