# 📈 Data Operations & Observability AI

Nine interlinked apps that keep data pipelines, metrics, APIs and cloud architectures healthy — part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md).

## Apps in this category

### [Data Catalog Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-catalog-copilot/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/data-catalog-copilot)
AI copilot for data catalogs — auto-documents tables, lineage, and ownership.

### [Data Quality Watchdog](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-quality-watchdog/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/data-quality-watchdog)
Detects schema drift, nulls, and anomalies across pipelines before they hit dashboards.

### [ETL Pipeline Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/etl-pipeline-monitor/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/etl-pipeline-monitor)
AI ETL/pipeline monitoring — predicts failures and ranks root causes.

### [Metric Anomaly Alerter](https://ziontechgroup.com/metric-anomaly-alerter/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/metric-anomaly-alerter)
Real-time metric anomaly detection and alerting with business context.

### [Dashboard Insight Narrator](https://ziontechgroup.com/dashboard-insight-narrator/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/dashboard-insight-narrator)
Turns dashboards into plain-language insight narratives for stakeholders.

### [Report Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/report-scheduler-ai/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/report-scheduler-ai)
AI report scheduling and distribution — right report, right person, right time.

### [API Health Probe](https://ziontechgroup.com/api-health-probe/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/api-health-probe)
Monitor API endpoints with response-time charts, uptime %, and alerts.

### [Cloud Cost Estimator](https://ziontechgroup.com/cloud-cost-estimator/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/cloud-cost-estimator)
Multi-cloud compute & serverless price comparison.

### [Cloud Architect Diagram Builder](https://ziontechgroup.com/cloud-architect-diagram-builder/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/cloud-architect-diagram-builder)
Drag-and-drop AWS/Azure/GCP architecture diagrams with SVG/PNG export.

## 🔗 How they interlink

- **[Data Catalog Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-catalog-copilot/)** documents your tables → **[Data Quality Watchdog](https://ziontechgroup.com/data-quality-watchdog/)** monitors them → **[ETL Pipeline Monitor](https://ziontechgroup.com/etl-pipeline-monitor/)** watches the pipelines feeding them.
- **[Metric Anomaly Alerter](https://ziontechgroup.com/metric-anomaly-alerter/)** spots business-metric anomalies → **[Dashboard Insight Narrator](https://ziontechgroup.com/dashboard-insight-narrator/)** explains them in plain language → **[Report Scheduler AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/report-scheduler-ai/)** delivers the narrative to stakeholders on schedule.
- **[API Health Probe](https://ziontechgroup.com/api-health-probe/)** tracks endpoint uptime → **[Cloud Architect Diagram Builder](https://ziontechgroup.com/cloud-architect-diagram-builder/)** maps the architecture → **[Cloud Cost Estimator](https://ziontechgroup.com/cloud-cost-estimator/)** prices it across clouds.
