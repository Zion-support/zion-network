# Batch 120 — Cybersecurity Operations AI

Seven interlinked, free & open-source AI apps for security teams, MSSPs and enterprise IT.
All apps cross-link to each other and to the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md). Free Discovery: https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/

## Apps
### soc-alert-triage-ai
SOC Alert Triage AI — auto-triage SIEM/EDR alerts: dedupe, enrich, score and route with false-positive suppression.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/soc-alert-triage-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/soc-alert-triage-ai
### threat-intel-aggregator-ai
Threat Intel Aggregator — fuse OSINT/commercial feeds into scored, deduplicated, actionable IOC briefs.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/threat-intel-aggregator-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/threat-intel-aggregator-ai
### vulnerability-prioritizer-ai
Vulnerability Prioritizer AI — rank CVEs by exploitability (KEV/EPSS), asset criticality and business context.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/vulnerability-prioritizer-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/vulnerability-prioritizer-ai
### incident-response-playbook-ai
Incident Response Playbook AI — generate executable IR runbooks per incident type with evidence checklists.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/incident-response-playbook-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/incident-response-playbook-ai
### ransomware-readiness-ai
Ransomware Readiness AI — assess backup/restore posture, segment gaps and tabletop-scenario scoring.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/ransomware-readiness-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/ransomware-readiness-ai
### identity-access-review-ai
Identity Access Review AI — automate user access reviews, dormant accounts and privilege creep detection.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/identity-access-review-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/identity-access-review-ai
### security-awareness-coach-ai
Security Awareness Coach AI — personalized micro-training from phishing simulations and incident patterns.
- Live: https://ziontechgroup.com/security-awareness-coach-ai/ · GitHub: https://github.com/Zion-support/security-awareness-coach-ai
