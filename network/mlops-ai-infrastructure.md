## Model lifecycle workflow
1. Prepare data with [AI Data Labeling](https://ai-data-labeling.ziontechgroup.com), [AI Data Pipeline](https://ai-data-pipeline.ziontechgroup.com) and [AI Data Validator](https://ai-data-validator.ziontechgroup.com).
2. Train and tune with [AI ML Pipeline](https://ai-ml-pipeline.ziontechgroup.com) and [AI Hyperparameter Tuner](https://ai-hyperparameter-tuner.ziontechgroup.com).
3. Evaluate with [AI Eval Harness](https://ziontechgroup.com/ai-eval-harness/).
4. Serve and route with [LLM Gateway](https://ziontechgroup.com/llm-gateway/) and deploy anywhere with [AI Edge Deployer](https://ai-edge-deployer.ziontechgroup.com).
5. Operate with [AI Cluster Manager](https://ai-cluster-manager.ziontechgroup.com), [AI Compute Optimizer](https://ai-compute-optimizer.ziontechgroup.com), [AI Cloud Optimizer](https://ai-cloud-optimizer.ziontechgroup.com) and [AI Infrastructure Monitor](https://ai-infrastructure-monitor.ziontechgroup.com).
6. Watch quality with [Model Observatory](https://ziontechgroup.com/model-observatory/) and [AI Data Viz](https://ai-data-viz.ziontechgroup.com).
