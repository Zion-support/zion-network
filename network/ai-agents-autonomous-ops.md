## Why autonomous agents

- **Plan → act → verify loops**: every agent logs decisions for auditability
- **Human-in-the-loop gates**: approval thresholds for high-impact actions
- **Composable**: agents share signals through the network (escalation sentinel feeds dispatch optimizer; churn radar feeds sales copilot)
