# 🛡️ Compliance & GRC AI

Eight interlinked apps for governance, risk and compliance teams — part of the [Zion App Network](../APPS_NETWORK.md).

## Apps in this category

### [Zion AI Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-compliance-scanner/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-compliance-scanner)
Automated compliance scanning — CIS benchmarks, policy enforcement, and audit reports.

### [Vendor Risk Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-risk-screener/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/vendor-risk-screener)
AI vendor risk screening — security, financial, and compliance signals scored.

### [Audit Readiness Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/audit-readiness-copilot/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/audit-readiness-copilot)
AI audit-readiness copilot — control gaps, evidence status, and auditor Q&A prep.

### [Policy Change Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/policy-change-tracker/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/policy-change-tracker)
AI regulatory & policy change tracking — alerts with impact summaries per jurisdiction.

### [Contract Clause Extractor](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-extractor/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/contract-clause-extractor)
AI contract clause extraction — pull obligations, dates, and risks from any contract.

### [Compliance Evidence Collector](https://ziontechgroup.com/compliance-evidence-collector/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/compliance-evidence-collector)
AI compliance evidence collection — SOC2/ISO evidence gathered continuously.

### [Zion AI Drift Detector](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-drift-detector/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-ai-drift-detector)
Configuration drift detection and auto-remediation for multi-vendor IT fleets.

### [SLA Forecast Guardian](https://ziontechgroup.com/sla-forecast-guardian/) · [repo](https://github.com/Zion-support/sla-forecast-guardian)
Predict SLA breaches before they happen and auto-escalate.

## 🔗 How they interlink

- **[Policy Change Tracker](https://ziontechgroup.com/policy-change-tracker/)** detects regulatory change → **[Zion AI Compliance Scanner](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-compliance-scanner/)** re-scans controls → **[Compliance Evidence Collector](https://ziontechgroup.com/compliance-evidence-collector/)** gathers SOC2/ISO proof continuously.
- **[Vendor Risk Screener](https://ziontechgroup.com/vendor-risk-screener/)** scores third parties → **[Contract Clause Extractor](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-clause-extractor/)** pulls obligations from their contracts → **[Audit Readiness Copilot](https://ziontechgroup.com/audit-readiness-copilot/)** keeps you audit-ready.
- **[Zion AI Drift Detector](https://ziontechgroup.com/zion-ai-drift-detector/)** remediates config drift → **[SLA Forecast Guardian](https://ziontechgroup.com/sla-forecast-guardian/)** predicts and escalates breaches.
